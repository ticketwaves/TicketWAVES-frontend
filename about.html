About TicketWAVES

TicketWAVES is a ticket marketplace built to help fans discover, buy, sell and transfer tickets for concerts, sports, theatre and live entertainment events.

Our goal is to make ticket management simple, secure and accessible. Users can manage their tickets, transfer ownership, and connect with other fans through a modern ticketing experience.

TicketWAVES continues to improve platform security, buyer protection and seller verification to create a safer marketplace for everyone.
