TicketWAVES Refund Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Overview

TicketWAVES is a ticket marketplace connecting buyers and sellers. Refund eligibility depends on event status, ticket validity, and seller compliance.

Cancelled Events

If an event is officially cancelled and not rescheduled, eligible buyers may receive a refund according to event organizer policies.

Postponed Events

Tickets generally remain valid for rescheduled dates unless otherwise announced by the organizer.

Invalid Tickets

If a buyer receives a ticket that is proven invalid and cannot be used for entry, TicketWAVES may investigate and provide protection under our Buyer Protection Policy.

Seller Responsibilities

Sellers must provide accurate ticket details and valid tickets.

Buyer Responsibilities

Buyers should carefully review listing information before purchasing.

Chargebacks

Fraudulent chargebacks may result in account suspension and legal action.
